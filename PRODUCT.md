# Corrigenda

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user: Next.js and TypeScript, with a server API, Neon PostgreSQL, Privy email authentication and GenLayer Python Intelligent Contracts. Server workers schedule and index signed transactions.

## Users
Oracle builders, audit attestation registries, publishers, and application owners who need to correct evidence already consumed by other applications.

## Product Purpose
Carry published corrections to the precise decisions that relied on them. Stop future actions from relying on materially withdrawn evidence and make remediation visible.

## Positioning
Proposition specific impact certificates, registered dependencies and acknowledged correction generations across applications.

## Operating Context
Owners register source revisions and dependencies. Validators independently compare authentic records. Owners inspect the source comparison, affected decisions and remediation queue. A durable dispatcher carries finalized certificates to consumer contracts.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Eight propositions per correction, twenty dependents per dispatch transaction, immutable increasing graph ranks, bounded authentic evidence and three application assessment attempts per revision. Historical irreversible payouts remain historical. Future authorization is quarantined until replacement evidence is accepted. The inbox is the first workspace, with source comparison and remediation beside it, as recommended and approved by the user.

## Brand Commitments
Corrigenda. Charcoal #131315, yellow #FED400, paper #F2F1EF. Original logo and favicon. Human readable language, proper icons, light and dark themes, purposeful transitions. No generic marketing scaffold, emoji icons, decorative uppercase labels or unnecessary hyphens in visible text.

## Evidence on Hand
No deployed contracts or registered records existed at project creation. Product data must come from the actual network. Controlled test fixtures must be labeled in verification records and must never appear as real adoption.

## Product Principles
Evidence is untrusted text. Authority is explicitly configured. Validator consensus owns semantic consequences. New actions check ancestor flags even before delivery. Authentication uses email and transaction details are handled behind the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts and reduced motion.
